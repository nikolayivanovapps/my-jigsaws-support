My Jigsaws Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Data in the app

"My Jigsaws" does not collect or send your personal data to the developer. Photos you choose are used to create puzzles on your device. The app stores puzzle images, progress, play statistics, and settings locally.

Your device backups may include app data, depending on your backup settings.

Contact
